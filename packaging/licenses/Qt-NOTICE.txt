Desktop Chibi bundles dynamically loaded Qt and Qt for Python (PySide6,
Shiboken) libraries from The Qt Company Ltd. and their contributors.
The LGPL v3 license texts are supplied here. Font licenses are included
under _internal/desktop_chibi/assets/fonts/ in the standalone bundle.

Qt for Python source and notices:
https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
https://github.com/pyside/pyside-setup

Qt source and notices:
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/

The exact installed library versions are recorded in build-*.json and
requirements-build.txt. Qt/PySide shared libraries remain separate files
under _internal/PySide6/ and can be replaced with compatible builds.
No modifications to Qt or PySide libraries are made by Desktop Chibi.

bootloader exception; see https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html.
